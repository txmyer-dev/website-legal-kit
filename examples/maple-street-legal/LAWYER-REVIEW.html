Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Maple Street Chiropractic ("Maple Street Chiropractic", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit example.com, call or text us, or request an appointment.

Your medical information. This policy covers our website and online contact only. How we use and protect your health information as a patient is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices [FILL: npp_url], as required by HIPAA.
For review: Covered entities must post their HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices prominently on their website (45 CFR 164.520(c)(3)(i)). The NPP is the practice's own document; we only link to it. Confirm the practice has a current NPP (updated for the 2024 reproductive-health and 2026 Part 2 changes, if applicable).

Information we collect

  • What you give us: your name, phone number, email address and anything you write in a form, email or text.
  • Appointment requests: the date, time and reason you enter when you ask for an appointment.
  • Website use: basic technical data such as browser, device, pages viewed and IP address, collected by our hosting provider to run and secure the site.

Please do not send detailed medical information through our website forms, email or text messages. Call us, or bring it to your visit.

For review: Website forms that collect a reason for visit may be PHI. Confirm the form vendor will sign a Business Associate Agreement, or limit the form to name/phone/preferred time. Per HHS OCR guidance on online tracking (partly vacated in AHA v. Becerra, N.D. Tex. 2024 for unauthenticated pages), we recommend NO advertising pixels (Meta, Google Ads, TikTok) on healthcare sites. Our default build has none.

How we use it

  • To respond to you, schedule and confirm appointments, and send the reminders you ask for.
  • To run, secure and improve our website.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

How we share it

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers that run our website, phones, email, scheduling and text messaging. They may use it only to provide services to us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of you, us or others.
  • As part of a sale or transfer of the practice, subject to this policy.
For review: Confirm 'we do not sell/share' is true for every tool on the site. If ad pixels are ever added, this section, a 'Do Not Sell or Share' link and a cookie notice must be added before they go live.

Text messages

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is shared only with the providers that deliver our messages (our messaging platform and phone carriers). Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every other sharing described in this policy.

If you agree to receive texts, you may receive appointment reminders, replies to your questions and office updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Text Messaging Terms.

For review: This exact sharing language is what carriers look for when approving business texting (A2P 10DLC registration). Changing it can cause the registration to be rejected.

Keeping and protecting information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

For review: Medical-record retention is governed separately (PA: 7 years after last visit for adults, longer for minors, under the State Board rules for the profession). This policy covers website data only.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, subject to legal and medical-record requirements.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies.
For review: State consumer-privacy laws (including Pennsylvania's, if enacted and in force by publication) generally apply above revenue/volume thresholds a small practice won't meet, and many exempt HIPAA-covered data. Confirm none apply to Maple Street Chiropractic and that no state-specific rights section is needed.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information online. Parents or guardians handle appointments for minors.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Accessibility Statement

Maple Street Chiropractic wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website and get the information and services it offers.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. That standard covers things like readable color contrast, text alternatives for images, full keyboard use, clear headings and labels, and pages that work with screen readers and zoom.

For review: We build to WCAG 2.2 AA and test with automated checks plus manual keyboard and screen-reader passes. The statement says 'aim to meet', not 'fully conforms': a conformance claim is a promise; a commitment with a working feedback channel is what courts and DOJ have treated as good faith. Counsel may prefer other wording.

What we've done

  • Built the site to WCAG 2.2 AA and tested it with automated tools, keyboard-only use and a screen reader.
  • Checked color contrast, text size, zoom to 200%, and layout on phones.
  • Given every image that carries meaning a text description, and every form field a label.
  • Avoided automatic carousels, flashing content and time limits.
  • Reviewed the site most recently on [FILL: a11y_review_date].

We do not use an accessibility overlay or widget. We fix the site itself instead.

Known limitations

We are not aware of any barriers at this time.

For review: List any real gaps (e.g. embedded maps, third-party booking widgets, PDF forms). Naming known gaps is better than a blanket claim. Our intake-form PDFs are tagged and fillable, but a phone alternative is still offered below.

Content from others

Some features come from other companies, such as maps, reviews or online booking. We choose accessible options where we can, and we will help you by phone with anything they make hard to use.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of this website is hard for you to use, please tell us and we'll help you get what you need another way, and work to fix the barrier.

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Our accessibility contact is Dr. Jordan Example.

Not medical advice

The information on this website is general and for education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not create a doctor-patient relationship. Talk with a qualified provider about your own health.

In an emergency, call 911. Do not use this website, email or text messages for urgent medical needs.

Accuracy and links

We try to keep the site accurate and current, including hours, services and insurance information, but it may contain errors or be out of date. Call us to confirm. Links to other websites are for convenience; we don't control them and aren't responsible for their content.

Limitation of liability

The site is provided as is. To the fullest extent the law allows, Maple Street Chiropractic is not liable for damages arising from use of the website. This does not limit any responsibility we have for the care we provide.

For review: Kept deliberately short and plain. Counsel may want stronger disclaimer/limitation language; note that over-broad disclaimers on a healthcare site can read poorly to patients and won't limit malpractice exposure anyway.

Text Messaging Terms

Program

Program name: Maple Street Chiropractic Patient Messages

Maple Street Chiropractic sends appointment confirmations and reminders, replies to your questions, and office updates (such as closures) by text to people who give us their mobile number and agree to receive texts.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opt-out

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel; you'll get one confirmation and no further messages. You can also call us to opt out.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Health information

Text messages are not a secure way to send medical details. Our texts are limited to scheduling and office information. Please don't text us symptoms or medical history.

For review: HIPAA permits appointment reminders by text when the patient has given the number and is told texts are unencrypted; keep reminder content to date/time/location. Confirm the messaging vendor signs a BAA.

Privacy

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase or of receiving care. See our Privacy Policy.

For review: Promotional texts (specials, reactivation campaigns) need prior express WRITTEN consent under the TCPA; appointment/service texts need prior express consent. The form-consent snippet covers both; confirm it fits what the practice will actually send.
