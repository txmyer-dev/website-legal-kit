Accessibility Statement

Maple Street Chiropractic wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website and get the information and services it offers.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. That standard covers things like readable color contrast, text alternatives for images, full keyboard use, clear headings and labels, and pages that work with screen readers and zoom.

What we've done

  • Built the site to WCAG 2.2 AA and tested it with automated tools, keyboard-only use and a screen reader.
  • Checked color contrast, text size, zoom to 200%, and layout on phones.
  • Given every image that carries meaning a text description, and every form field a label.
  • Avoided automatic carousels, flashing content and time limits.
  • Reviewed the site most recently on [FILL: a11y_review_date].

We do not use an accessibility overlay or widget. We fix the site itself instead.

Known limitations

We are not aware of any barriers at this time.

Content from others

Some features come from other companies, such as maps, reviews or online booking. We choose accessible options where we can, and we will help you by phone with anything they make hard to use.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of this website is hard for you to use, please tell us and we'll help you get what you need another way, and work to fix the barrier.
